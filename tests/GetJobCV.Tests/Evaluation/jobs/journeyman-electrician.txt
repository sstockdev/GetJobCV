Journeyman Electrician - Commercial
Iron Range Electric · Hibbing, MN

Iron Range Electric is a union electrical contractor doing commercial and light industrial work across northern Minnesota. We need Journeyman Electricians for new construction and remodel projects.

Responsibilities
- Install, maintain, and repair electrical systems in commercial buildings
- Read and work from blueprints and electrical drawings
- Bend and install EMT and rigid conduit
- Pull wire, terminate, and install panels, switchgear, and lighting
- Troubleshoot circuits and equipment with a multimeter
- Wire motor controls and three-phase equipment
- Follow lockout/tagout and job site safety rules

Requirements
- Active Minnesota Journeyman Electrician license
- 4+ years of commercial electrical experience
- Working knowledge of the National Electrical Code (NEC)
- OSHA 10 certification
- Own hand tools and reliable transportation

Preferred
- OSHA 30
- Experience with fire alarm or low voltage systems
- PLC experience
