Account Executive - Packaging Solutions
Northstar Packaging Supply · Minneapolis, MN

Northstar Packaging Supply sells corrugated boxes, stretch film, and packaging equipment to manufacturers and distributors across the Upper Midwest. We're hiring an Account Executive to grow our customer base in the Twin Cities territory.

Responsibilities
- Prospect for new accounts through cold calling, email, and LinkedIn
- Manage a pipeline of opportunities in Salesforce and keep forecasts current
- Run discovery meetings and present solutions to purchasing and operations managers
- Prepare quotes, negotiate pricing, and close deals
- Grow existing accounts and build long-term customer relationships
- Meet or exceed a quarterly sales quota

Qualifications
- 3+ years of B2B outside sales experience
- Proven track record of hitting quota
- Experience with a CRM, preferably Salesforce
- Strong negotiation and presentation skills
- Excellent communication and time management
- Valid driver's license; up to 50% local travel

Preferred
- Experience selling to manufacturers or in the packaging or industrial supply industry
- Familiarity with consultative or solution selling
- Bachelor's degree in Business or Marketing
