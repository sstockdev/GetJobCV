Registered Nurse - Medical/Surgical Unit
St. Adalbert Hospital · Duluth, MN

St. Adalbert Hospital is seeking a Registered Nurse for our 32-bed medical/surgical unit. Full time, rotating shifts.

Responsibilities
- Provide direct patient care for 4 to 6 patients per shift
- Administer medications and IV therapy
- Assess patients, monitor vital signs, and document care in Epic
- Educate patients and families on discharge plans
- Collaborate with physicians and the care team

Requirements
- Active Minnesota RN license
- BLS certification required; ACLS within 6 months of hire
- 2+ years of acute care nursing experience
- Proficiency with electronic health records, preferably Epic
- Strong communication and critical thinking skills

Preferred
- BSN
- Medical/surgical or telemetry experience
