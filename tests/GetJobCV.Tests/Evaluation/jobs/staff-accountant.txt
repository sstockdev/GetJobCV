Staff Accountant
Cedar Valley Manufacturing · Cedar Rapids, IA

Cedar Valley Manufacturing makes agricultural equipment parts and is growing fast. Our accounting team is looking for a Staff Accountant to support month-end close and financial reporting.

Responsibilities
- Prepare journal entries and account reconciliations
- Support month-end and year-end close
- Maintain the general ledger in NetSuite
- Process accounts payable and accounts receivable
- Prepare financial statements and variance analysis for management
- Help with the annual audit

Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Accounting or Finance
- 2+ years of accounting experience
- Knowledge of GAAP
- Advanced Excel skills, including pivot tables and VLOOKUP
- Strong attention to detail

Preferred
- CPA or working toward one
- Experience with NetSuite or QuickBooks
