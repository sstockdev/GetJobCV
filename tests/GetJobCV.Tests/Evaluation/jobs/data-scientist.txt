Data Scientist
Contoso Health Analytics · Remote (US)

Contoso Health Analytics helps hospital systems predict patient readmissions. We are hiring a Data Scientist to build and evaluate predictive models and to share findings with clinical and business stakeholders.

Responsibilities
- Build, validate, and monitor machine learning models for readmission risk
- Clean and explore large datasets with Python and SQL
- Design and analyze A/B tests of care interventions
- Build dashboards and present results to non-technical audiences

Minimum Qualifications
- 3+ years of experience in a data science or analytics role
- Strong Python, including pandas, NumPy, and scikit-learn
- Strong SQL
- Solid grounding in statistics and machine learning
- Experience with one of the following: Tableau, Power BI, or Looker
- Strong communication skills

Nice to have
- Experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Spark or other big data tools
- Healthcare data experience is a plus
