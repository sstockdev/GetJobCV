High School Mathematics Teacher
Lakeview Public Schools, ISD 412 · Brainerd, MN

Lakeview High School is looking for a Mathematics Teacher for grades 9-12 starting in the fall semester. Courses include Algebra I, Geometry, and Algebra II.

Responsibilities
- Plan and deliver lessons aligned with the Minnesota Academic Standards in Mathematics
- Use differentiated instruction to reach students at every level
- Create formative and summative assessments and use the data to adjust instruction
- Maintain a positive classroom with clear expectations and consistent classroom management
- Follow IEPs and 504 plans in partnership with special education staff
- Communicate with parents and guardians about student progress
- Post assignments and grades in Google Classroom and Infinite Campus

Qualifications
- Minnesota teaching license in 5-12 Mathematics, or eligible
- Bachelor's degree in Mathematics or Mathematics Education
- Student teaching or classroom experience at the secondary level
- Strong communication and collaboration skills

Preferred
- Experience teaching AP Calculus or AP Statistics
- Experience with PBIS
- Coaching or advising an extracurricular activity
